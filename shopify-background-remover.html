Shopify image guidelines

Verified Shopify product image guidance

These are Shopify's current technical limits and display recommendations. Your theme, product category, and store design can still call for different creative choices.

📐
Size & Resolution
  • 2048×2048 px recommended (square)
  • Up to 5000×5000 px or 25 megapixels
  • File must be smaller than 20 MB
  • Use consistent aspect ratios for aligned grids
🎨
Store consistency
  • Choose white, transparent, or a branded backdrop
  • Keep the same aspect ratio across a product grid
  • Preview the result in your current theme
  • Shopify does not require a fixed product fill ratio
📁
File Format
  • JPG (best for white backgrounds)
  • PNG (if you need transparency)
  • WebP supported
  • GIF for animated product views

FAQ

Common questions from Shopify sellers

Does the Shopify preset guarantee listing compliance?
No tool can guarantee how every theme or product page will look. The preset uses Shopify's recommended 2048×2048 square size, supports accepted PNG, JPEG, and WebP formats, and blocks outputs that are not under 20 MB. Review the final image in your store before publishing.
Can I process all my product photos in one go?
Yes. You can upload up to 50 images per batch. All images are processed in parallel and delivered as a single ZIP file, ready to bulk-upload to Shopify.
Does it work for clothing, jewelry, and shoes?
Absolutely. The AI handles complex product shapes including apparel on mannequins, jewelry with fine details, footwear, and accessories — all common categories for Shopify stores.
