Marketplace image policies

Verified Amazon & eBay image guidance

The presets apply supported dimensions, formats, and available file-size checks. They do not guarantee listing approval; always review category-specific rules before upload.

🟠
Amazon MAIN Image Policy
  • Pure white background — RGB (255, 255, 255)
  • Product occupies at least 85% of the image area
  • No text, logos, or watermarks
  • 1600 px or more on the longest side is optimal for zoom
  • At least 1000 px on the longest side enables zoom
  • Maximum 10,000 px on longest side
  • JPEG, PNG, GIF, or TIFF format
The app disables WebP for Amazon and recommends JPEG. Category-specific MAIN image rules can be stricter.
🔴
eBay Listing Image Standards
  • 1600×1600 px recommended; 500 px minimum
  • White or neutral background preferred
  • No borders, watermarks, or overlaid text
  • Product clearly visible and well-lit
  • Up to 24 photos per listing
  • JPEG, PNG, GIF, TIFF, BMP, WebP, HEIC, or AVIF
  • Maximum 12 MB per uploaded image
The app accepts PNG, JPEG, and WebP for eBay and blocks outputs over 12 MB.

FAQ

Questions from Amazon & eBay sellers

Will this prevent my Amazon listing from being suppressed?
No. The Amazon preset helps with size and supported formats, and you can choose a pure-white background, but the app cannot verify every product-fill, content, or category-specific rule. Review the final MAIN image and current category guidance before uploading.
Can I process an entire Amazon catalog at once?
Yes — upload up to 50 images per batch. For large catalogs, you can run multiple batches. All output images are delivered as a single ZIP file named to match your originals.
Does it work for FBA product photos taken at home?
Yes. You can replace a desk, floor, or colored backdrop with white. You still need to review the cutout, product framing, and category rules before upload.
What about eBay's multiple image requirement?
eBay allows up to 24 images per listing. You can batch process all angle shots and variations at once, getting a full set of clean white background images for each listing in one go.
